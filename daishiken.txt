DAISHIKEN QUIZES

1. The Other One

There will be 5 slots of letters, and 2 types of question.
This 5 letters will resemble a group of the same -gyo or the same -dan.

Type 1 is where you have to choose which letter isn't belong in the group.
Ex:
Ka Ki Du Ke Ko

Answer: Du

Or

Ra Da Ta Tu Pa

Answer: Tu

Type 2 is where the will be an empty spot in the group.
You have to choose a letter that fit the group.

Ex:
Ka Ki __ Ke Ko

Answer: Ku

Or

Pa Da Na _ Sa

Answer: Ma

2. Wordbuilder

There will be like maybe 100 or more letters presented in a grid.
There will be a goal word that you have to build using the letters provided.
Romaji is provided as the hint. The meaning of the word is also provided.
Letter that has been chosen can't be used anymore and disappear.
The goal is making the grid empty.

3. Under Guidance

There will be four short sentences that shares the same structure in kana.
Some words in the sentences is shared on the same location between sentences.
The meaning of each sentences is provided.
Then there will be a sentence but only the meaning.
This sentence will also share the same structure and take words from previous 4 sentences.
Choose between 4 options which sentence fits the meaning.

Ex (in kana):
Dog under a chair
Dog on a chair
Bird under a table
Bird on a chair

Question
Dog on a table

Options in kana

4. Textbuilder

There will be a short text, but all of them is blank. Romaji is provided as a hint.
The text will approximately contains 8 sentences. The meaning of the whole text is provided.
There will be 4 options of sentences for each sentences in kana.
The sentences is answered in order, one by one.
