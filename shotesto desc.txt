1 Check the truth

similar to truth or false
there will be 6 pairs of kana and romaji
and also 1 statement
player have to check if the statement is true o or false x
the statement will be a number of pair that is true or false

example
Satu pasangan salah
Semua pasangan benar
5 pasangan salah
tidak ada pasangan yang benar
semua pasangan salah

false statement should have the number miss 1 from the truth
if the truth is 2 wrong
then false statement should be 1 wrong or 3 wrong

2 Fill the trace

similar to old sequence drill
there will be 7 spots of underscore
the memory phase is the kana one by one appears
from left to right in a certain interval
but on the 5th spot, the 1st kana disappear
and on the 6th spot, the 2nd kana disappear
and on the 7th spot, the 3rd kana disappear
so on until the 7th kana disappear

after that one spot will randomly highlighted
and the whole 15 letters from previous lesson will be there in randomly
they have to fill the highlighted spot with the correct kana
based on the trace they remember

3 Flip it!

typical memory game
there will be 30 squares, 15 kana 15 romaji
randomly shuffled in the grid
they'll have to click the square to flip
the objective is to find the matching pairs
if the kana and the romaji match, they have to click a button "match"
only then the pairs will be removed and get a point
lose a live if actually mismatch
once the third square is flipped, the previous two square will be flipped back

4 Quick Glance

there will be 60 pairs of kana and romaji that will formed in 4x15 grid
there will be another bar that is red and full in 7 seconds
the objective is to find a match every 7 seconds or before the bar full
there will be 6 pairs that will be match
and every correct answer will randomly change 10 pairs
including the selected one into a new pair
while keep the composition of 6 pairs that match
so the other true answer can also be changed
but there has to be another correct answer somewhere else
